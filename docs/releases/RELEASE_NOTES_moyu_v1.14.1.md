# Clock Out Alive / 摸鱼到下班 v1.14.1

v1.14.1 is a draw-layer art pass. It does not change the accepted runner rules, obstacle dimensions, collision rectangles, speed curve or route length.

## Hazard presentation

- Boss gets a readable side-running office silhouette with suit, tie, glasses, briefcase, stride and rush posture.
- BUG gains a clearer two-part body, animated legs, antennae/eyes and mutation-warning treatment.
- Temporary requests become a stacked document/clipboard drop with an ASAP stamp while retaining the existing warning and falling states.
- Mail, coffee spills and dumbbells receive cleaner silhouettes and materials without changing their physics footprints.
- Meeting gates remain geometry-first so the safe opening stays visually unambiguous.

## Hero polish

- The existing v1.14.0 sprite sheet now uses frame 8 as the dedicated first-takeoff pose before the ascent frames.
- Jump velocities, Double Jump behavior, 44×66 player body and collision geometry are unchanged.

## Release contract

The release tests and deploy gates require the new hazard render functions. The hero PNG remains the exact reviewed v1.14.0 binary and keeps its independent asset generation/checksum.
