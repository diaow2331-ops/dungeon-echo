电脑：A/D 或 ←/→ 移动，Space/W 跳跃，Q 闪避，S/↓ 下攀，左键连续采矿/攻击；装备弓时会消耗骨木箭进行远程射击，右键连续放置，滚轮或 1–8 切快捷栏，Ctrl 切换智能光标，Shift 临时智能选工具，E 背包。

Desktop: A/D or arrows move, Space/W jumps, Q dodges, S/Down descends ropes, hold left to mine/attack; bows spend Woodbone Arrows for ranged shots, hold right to place, wheel or 1–8 cycles hotbar, Ctrl toggles Smart Cursor, Shift temporarily auto-selects the best tool, E opens pack.

手机横屏：左摇杆移动，向上推可跳跃/攀绳；右摇杆直接瞄准并连续采掘或攻击，放置键使用当前瞄准方向；再次对准已建路标并使用放置键可拆除回收；火把键无需切栏即可快速照明。背包键打开制造。深入星晶裂隙后，对准星核炉长按采/战键即可点燃。

Landscape touch: left stick moves; push up to jump or climb. Right stick aims and continuously mines/attacks. Place uses the current aim; Torch gives quick lighting without changing slots. Pack opens crafting. In the Starshard Rift, hold Mine/Use on the Starcore Forge to ignite it.

夜幕期间会进入守夜高压，熔火堆附近会形成守护区并抑制普通荒兽；靠近工匠台与熔火堆可绑定前哨。夜里熔火堆回复更快，天亮会提示熬夜结果。

At night, threat pressure rises. An Ember Pit creates a ward that suppresses ordinary creatures nearby; keeping it near a Craft Table lets you bind an outpost. Healing is faster at night, and dawn reports the night-watch result.
