电脑：A/D 或 ←/→ 移动，Space/W 跳跃，Q 闪避，R 放下/收起货包，S/↓ 下攀，左键连续采矿/攻击；装备弓时会消耗骨木箭进行远程射击，右键连续放置，滚轮或 1–8 切快捷栏，Ctrl 切换智能光标，Shift 临时智能选工具，E 背包。

Desktop: A/D or arrows move, Space/W jumps, Q dodges, R sets down/recovers cargo, S/Down descends ropes, hold left to mine/attack; bows spend Woodbone Arrows for ranged shots, hold right to place, wheel or 1–8 cycles hotbar, Ctrl toggles Smart Cursor, Shift temporarily auto-selects the best tool, E opens pack.

手机横屏：左摇杆移动，向上推可跳跃/攀绳；右摇杆直接瞄准并连续采掘或攻击，放置键使用当前瞄准方向；再次对准已建路标并使用放置键可拆除回收；火把键无需切栏即可快速照明。背包键打开制造。深入星晶裂隙后，对准星核炉长按采/战键即可点燃。

Landscape touch: left stick moves; push up to jump or climb. Right stick aims and continuously mines/attacks. Place uses the current aim; Torch gives quick lighting without changing slots. Pack opens crafting. In the Starshard Rift, hold Mine/Use on the Starcore Forge to ignite it.

夜幕期间会进入守夜高压，熔火堆附近会形成守护区并抑制普通荒兽；靠近工匠台与熔火堆可绑定前哨。相邻路标会形成可见的补给信号线，沿线移动更快、夜间遭遇略少；跨地貌连接还会把另一片区域的基础物资带入路标补给。靠近路标可打开贸易页：工匠台把本地原料打包成标准货物并存入路标仓库，装货后受货运容量限制；重载会拖慢移动并在夜间提高遭遇压力。背负标准货物时无法正常战斗，可按 R/货包键把货物放下再反击；地上货包会在混战中受损。高价值货运更容易招来灰披劫徒，货站可用铸印雇佣临时护卫。商路与货运背架能显著降低风险。

At night, threat pressure rises. An Ember Pit wards ordinary creatures and can bind an outpost near a Craft Table. Nearby beacons form visible supply signals: travel is faster along them, night encounters ease slightly, and cross-biome links add a small exchange good to dawn supplies. Trade beside beacons: pack local materials into standardized cargo, store and load within capacity, then deliver it into distant demand. Heavy loads slow travel and raise off-route night pressure. You cannot fight normally while carrying standardized cargo: set the load down with R/CARGO, then defend it. Ground cargo can be damaged in crossfire; high-value loads attract rare Greyveil raiders, and Frontier Depots can hire temporary guards.
