100-Floor Journey

Every character starts on Floor 1. Each tenth floor is a major guardian checkpoint and Floor 100 is the production finale; shorter profiles exist only for internal testing.

Greedy Expedition

Depart → loot → return safely → prepare → go deeper.
Death loses backpack items and carried Gold; stash and vault are safe. Deeper gear is stronger, but deciding when to keep pushing and when to retreat is the central risk.

Combat

The four classes use different survival and damage rhythms. Gear is not only a score: ATK, DEF, HP, Crit, Leech, Thorns, Kill Heal and Gold Find have different value for different builds.
