关于 91hwl

91hwl 是一个独立维护的浏览器游戏站点，当前提供 Dungeon Echo（地牢回响）与 Clock Out Alive（摸鱼到下班）。两款游戏都以“打开即玩”为原则，不要求安装启动器或创建账号。

Dungeon Echo 是 1→100 层的回合制 Roguelike；Clock Out Alive 是约四分钟的办公室反应跑酷。项目持续根据实际游玩反馈迭代。

开放开发

Dungeon Echo 的源码、版本记录、发行说明与问题追踪公开在 GitHub。技术问题、可复现 Bug 与改进建议都可以通过公开仓库反馈。

About 91hwl

91hwl is an independently maintained browser-game site currently hosting Dungeon Echo and Clock Out Alive. Both are designed around a simple rule: open the page and play, with no launcher installation or account wall.

Dungeon Echo is a turn-based 1→100-floor roguelike. Clock Out Alive is a roughly four-minute office reaction run. Both continue to evolve from real play feedback.

Open development

Dungeon Echo source code, release history, engineering notes and issue tracking are public on GitHub. Reproducible bugs and technical feedback can be reported there.
