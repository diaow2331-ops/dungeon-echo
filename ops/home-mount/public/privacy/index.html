隐私Privacy

91hwl 的本地存储、访问日志、Google AdSense 与同意管理说明。How 91hwl handles local storage, access logs, Google AdSense and consent.

我们处理什么数据

91hwl 的游戏不要求注册账号。游戏进度与偏好主要保存在当前浏览器的本地存储中。站点可能使用 Cookie 或本地存储保存语言、主题等界面偏好。

服务器可能为安全、故障排查和基础运营记录标准访问日志，例如请求时间、IP 地址、浏览器/设备信息与请求路径。

Google AdSense 与同意管理

91hwl.cn 的部分非游戏页面接入 Google AdSense。Google 及其合作方可能依据用户所在地区、同意选择和适用法律使用 Cookie 或其他标识符提供、衡量和保护广告服务。针对欧洲经济区、英国与瑞士访客，站点使用 Google 的 CMP 提供同意、拒绝和管理选项。

play.91hwl.cn 的游戏运行界面不以展示广告为目的；当前广告商业化计划集中在 91hwl.cn 的站点页面。

第三方链接

站点包含 GitHub、X 等第三方链接。离开 91hwl 后，相应第三方的隐私政策和服务条款适用。

本政策会随站点功能或合规要求更新。最后更新：2026-08-29。

Data we handle

91hwl games do not require an account. Game progress and preferences are primarily stored locally in the current browser. The site may use cookies or local storage for interface preferences such as language and theme.

Standard server access logs may be retained for security, troubleshooting and basic operations, including request time, IP address, browser/device information and requested path.

Google AdSense and consent

Some non-game pages on 91hwl.cn are connected to Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or other identifiers to provide, measure and protect advertising services according to the visitor’s region, consent choices and applicable law. For visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the site uses Google’s CMP to provide consent, do-not-consent and manage-options choices.

The playable interfaces on play.91hwl.cn are not intended as advertising surfaces; current monetization plans are limited to the 91hwl.cn site pages.

Third-party links

The site links to third-party services such as GitHub and X. Their own privacy policies and terms apply after leaving 91hwl.

This policy may be updated as site features or compliance requirements change. Last updated: 2026-08-29.
