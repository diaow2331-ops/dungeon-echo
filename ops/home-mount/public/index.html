Product standard

这次统一治理了什么What the unified release means

双语是一等功能Bilingual by design

主页、详情页和游戏本体都使用同一套中文 / English 入口，不再出现“网站能切语言、游戏不能”的割裂。Home, detail pages and both games expose coherent Chinese / English paths instead of treating localization as a landing-page-only feature.

电脑优先，不牺牲手机Desktop-first, mobile-complete

PC 保留宽屏信息密度；手机则使用直接触控、安全区和窄屏布局，而不是简单把桌面界面缩小。Desktop keeps useful density; mobile gets direct input, safe-area handling and layouts designed for narrow screens instead of scaled-down desktop UI.

静态发布，可回滚Static and rollbackable

游戏、项目页和首页都走带校验和的静态 bundle，并在公开健康检查通过后才视为发布完成。Games, project pages and the homepage ship as checksummed static bundles and only count as released after public health checks pass.
