下潜不是唯一目标Descending is only half the decision

真正的长局压力来自：什么时候继续贪，什么时候把构筑安全带回城镇。The long-run tension comes from knowing when to push deeper and when to secure your build back in town.

01 / BUILD

构筑，而不是只看总分Builds, not one universal score

六装备栏、职业适配、Epic / Legendary 行为词缀，以及 +1～+5 锻造、+3 精炼和 +5 淬炼。Six gear slots, class-relative fit, Epic/Legendary behavior traits, +1–+5 forging, +3 refinement and +5 masterwork.

02 / RISK

继续贪，还是回城Push your luck or return

贪婪远征中，随身战利品和金币有风险；仓库与金库安全。撤退本身就是构筑的一部分。In Greedy Expedition, carried loot and gold are at risk while storage and banked value stay safe. Retreat is part of the build.

03 / COUNTERPLAY

Boss 用机制，不靠隐藏惩罚Readable bosses over hidden punishment

10 / 20 / … / 100 的守卫节点使用明确预警、位移、打断与阶段机制；第 100 层是三阶段终局。Guardian nodes at 10 / 20 / … / 100 use visible tells, movement, interrupts and phase mechanics. Floor 100 is a three-phase finale.

百层成长100-floor progression

所有职业都从第 1 层出发，不提供付费或开局跳层。成长来自构筑、技能进化和你真正征服过的深度。Every class starts on floor 1. There are no paid or opening skips into unconquered content. Progress comes from builds, skill evolution and depth you actually earned.

为什么是浏览器游戏Why a browser game

Dungeon Echo 保持为原生 HTML / CSS / JavaScript 静态应用：无需安装、无需账号、核心玩法没有运行时后端依赖。打开链接就能开始，存档留在当前浏览器。Dungeon Echo stays a vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript static app: no install, no account and no runtime backend dependency for core play. Open the link and start; saves remain in your browser.
