Core loop

三步就能理解The loop in three steps

不是自动战斗，也不是数值挂机。Not an idle game, not auto-combat.

01 / DESCEND

深入Descend

从第 1 层开始，探索、战斗、拿装备，逐步推进到 100 层。Start at floor 1, explore, fight, loot and work toward floor 100.

02 / BUILD

构筑Build

职业、装备、词缀、技能进化和 Mana 节奏共同决定打法。Class, gear, affixes, skill evolution and Mana shape the run.

03 / RETREAT

撤退Retreat

带战利品回城，存进安全仓库，再决定下一次要贪多深。Bank the loot in town, then decide how greedy the next descent should be.

v1.2.3

最终设备与视觉收尾Final device and visual cleanup

这一版没有重新做数值，而是修掉真人设备测试暴露出的最后几处体验缺陷。This patch does not retune balance. It closes the last device-facing defects found in human testing.

  • PC / 手机移除主角常驻光圈与技能就绪环。Removed the always-on player halo and skill-ready ring on desktop and mobile.
  • 手机 15×15 / 17×17 视口的视觉叠加层正确跟随相机。Visual overlays now follow the 15×15 / 17×17 mobile camera correctly.
  • 非全屏手机浏览器减少上下抖动与额外合成压力。Reduced non-fullscreen mobile browser jitter and composition pressure.
  • 四向方向键改为更快的 pointer-down 响应，中央等待键从触屏方向盘移除。Four-way movement reacts on pointer-down; the accidental center Wait target is gone.
Combat contract

J attacks, K skills and Mana

攻击与职业技能职责分离，四职业拥有不同 Mana 上限、消耗和回复节奏；等待仍保留在 PC 键盘，手机方向盘只负责移动。Attack and skill inputs are explicit. Each class has its own Mana capacity, cost and recovery rhythm. Keyboard Wait remains available on desktop; the mobile D-pad stays movement-only.
